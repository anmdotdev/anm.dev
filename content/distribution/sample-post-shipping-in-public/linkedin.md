This is a sample post, but the point is real.

Publishing systems stay honest only when something actually ships through them.

Private drafts optimize for comfort.
Public posts optimize for clarity.

The moment writing gets a URL, vague sentences stop hiding and half-finished arguments become obvious. That feedback loop is the whole advantage of shipping in public.

The useful habit is not "publish every day."

It is keeping the path from idea to published page short enough that friction does not become the editor.
